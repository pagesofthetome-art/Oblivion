Spell concept · Oblivion (2006) · playable preview

Crimson Grasp

A bandit camp outside an Ayleid ruin. Hold the spell on the bandit, the crates, the barrel or anything on the table to drag it to your hand. Turn and it swings after you. Let go and it's tossed a short way.

DEMO · touch, click or press any button to take over
Drag to look. Hold R1, Space or right mouse to grip. F throws, Q drops, WASD walks, Shift runs, R resets.
Tuning and debug

How it works in game

  • Hold, don't fire. R1 casts the grab, and a script keeps it alive for as long as R1 is down. It reads the button through NorthernUI's XXNIsGamepadKeyPressed.
  • Sweep to catch. While R1 is down and you hold nothing, the spell grabs the first thing your crosshair passes over.
  • People go limp. A held person or creature is paralyzed into a ragdoll, and gets back up a few seconds after you let go. Objects stay ordinary physics objects.
  • Real physics. The target is pulled toward a point in front of your hand, so it lags, swings and knocks into things as you turn. A hard throw into someone knocks them down.
  • Magicka, no timer. Holding drains magicka each second. Run dry and you lose your grip, with the same short toss.
  • New hand animation. Arm thrust out with the palm open, a tightening claw while holding, a flick on release.
Still to prove in game: steering ragdolls with steady pushes, and the best way to move objects (possibly through the game's own Telekinesis). This page uses a simple physics engine in place of Oblivion's Havok. Use the tuning drawer to try other values, and tell me the numbers you like.
